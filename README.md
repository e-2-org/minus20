# minus20

minus20 presents nine new web-based artworks selected by critic Sacha Craddock, Jon Thomson (of artist duo Thomson & Craighead [[www.thomson-craighead.net](https://www.thomson-craighead.net/)]) and John Wyver, Chair of Illuminations 
[[www.illumin.co.uk](https://web.archive.org/web/20120923232336/http://www.illumin.co.uk/)]

The aim of minus20 was to focus upon works of powerful immediacy with a fast download time - hence all the works had to be less than 20k in file size. This challenge seems to have captured artist’s imaginations with submissions being sent in from around the world and from a wide range of entrants - both artists who usually work with technology and those making a digital work for the first time.

Engagingly concise, the artworks were selected less for their technological innovation and more for their merit as artworks.
## Layout

The original files the site used are at the top of this repository: its pages and images, and the nine artworks in `projects/`. The open submissions to the commission aren't included. The current version of the site is in `docs/`, which is what GitHub Pages publishes. It only contains the files the site uses: the pages, their images and the nine artworks, with HTML ports of the Flash and Shockwave pieces in `docs/projects/port/`. The build scripts for the ports are in `_scripts/`. `_port/` has the notes on how the ports were made, the decompiled Lingo and ActionScript they were ported from, and videos of the Flash pieces. Summaries and transcripts of the Claude Code sessions are in `_sessions/`.
